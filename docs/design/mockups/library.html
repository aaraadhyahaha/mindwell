Chapter 2

Talking back to your thoughts

Thoughts are guesses, not facts. Check them like a fair judge, and aim for accurate rather than positive.

What it adds to mindwell

  • Thought records, started from any capture
  • The three Ps check
  • Before and after trends for each story

The evidence

Cognitive restructuring is the core of CBT (Hofmann et al., 2012). Unbelievable affirmations can backfire (Wood et al., 2009). Coaching yourself by name helps under stress (Kross et al., 2014).
