# ch02 — Chapter 2: Talking back to your thoughts (Thought records)

**Idea:** Thoughts are guesses, not facts. Check them like a fair judge and replace them with something accurate, not merely positive.
**Evidence:** Cognitive restructuring is the core of CBT, well supported across anxiety and depression (Hofmann et al., 2012). Permanent / pervasive / personal explanations predict giving up (Seligman). Unbelievable affirmations can backfire (Wood et al., 2009). Self-talk using your own name improves calm and performance (Kross et al., 2014).
**References:** Hofmann et al. 2012; Seligman 1990; Wood, Perunovic & Lee 2009; Kross et al. 2014.

## What the user does
Fills a thought record **fully in the app** (they may also copy it into their journal):
1. Situation
2. Automatic thought, word for word (pre-filled when started from a ch01 capture)
3. Feeling (word) + intensity 0–100
4. Evidence for
5. Evidence against — helper: "What would you tell a friend who thought this?"
6. Three-Ps check: toggles *permanent* / *pervasive* / *personal*; each toggled one shows a rewrite hint ("Make it temporary: 'right now…'")
7. Balanced thought — must be something you believe; coach line: "{name}, what's true and what's one next step?"
8. Feeling intensity now 0–100

Draft autosaves; a record counts as complete when steps 2, 3 and 8 are filled.

Via `ctx.signals` (signals) and the screen's `signals` snapshot (UI), never ch01's tables: `ch01.captures_recent` for the capture text and `story_id` when started from `?capture=<id>`; `ch01.stories` for story names; `ch01.story_counts_7d` for `unchallenged_story`. With ch01 inactive these are absent: no prefill, stories show no names, and `unchallenged_story` is not published.

## Screens and Today cards
- Screen `/m/ch02/new` (optional `?capture=<id>`): the stepped form above, one step per view, keyboard friendly.
- Screen `/m/ch02`: history; per-story chart of `intensity_before` vs `intensity_after` over time.
- Today card (anytime): "Start a thought record".
- ch01's evening card shows a "Thought record" action only via a link to `/m/ch02/new?capture=<id>` (route link, not an import).
