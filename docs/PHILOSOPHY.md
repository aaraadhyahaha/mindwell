# Philosophy

mindwell is an **evidence ledger, not a motivation source**.

Motivation fades within days. A record of your own thoughts, experiments and kept intentions becomes more useful every month you keep it. That is the reason to keep opening the app. After three months it can show what nothing else can: which negative stories keep returning and whether they're weakening, how much a thought record usually lowers your distress, and which techniques actually worked for you. This is self-efficacy built the way Chapter 12 of the book describes: from real evidence, not affirmations.

## The six principles

Check every feature against these before building it. If a feature breaks one, it doesn't ship.

1. **Evidence over motivation.** No quotes, badges, confetti, streak fireworks or celebration animations. The reward is seeing your own record.
2. **Every feature traces to the book.** Each module shows its chapter, the idea and the research behind it ("Why this exists"). No chapter, no feature.
3. **Every practice is an experiment** (Introduction). Techniques are tried for a set period, then kept or dropped based on how they worked for you.
4. **Suggests, you decide.** The app proposes prompts, targets and changes. Nothing changes without the user's approval.
5. **Kind about slips** (Chapter 11). No broken streaks, red warnings or guilt copy. A missed day is information. "Never miss twice" replaces streak pressure.
6. **Partner to the paper journal.** The app sets the day's intent and decides what is worth writing about. Free writing happens in the notebook. The app keeps structured data only: ratings, captured thoughts, thought records, intents, experiment verdicts.

## Tone of all copy

Plain, warm, short, second person. Like a calm coach, never a cheerleader or a scold.

- Yes: "Missed yesterday. What's the smallest step today?"
- No: "You broke your 12-day streak!" · "You've got this!! 🔥"

## Time budget

Morning check-in and evening close: under 3 minutes each. Total time with app + journal: 10–20 minutes a day. If a feature makes the daily loop longer, it needs a strong reason.
